Ch. 2The build

4 steps, then it's yours.

Two to four weeks, one person, no account management layer between you and the work.

01 — Audit

I go through what you have, what it costs you monthly, and what's actually losing you enquiries. You get the findings whether or not we work together.

02 — Build

Design and code in one pass, on your own hosting from day one. You watch it take shape on a live staging link, not in a slide deck.

03 — Review

Two rounds of changes, marked up directly on the page. Copy, layout, photography: we settle it before launch, not after.

04 — Handover

Repo, domain, hosting, analytics and a short video walkthrough, all in your name. Support if you want it, never as a condition.
