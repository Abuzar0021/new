Ch. 4Questions
Asked before

What does working with Pear cost?

There's no upfront charge and no hourly rate. We cover the cost of strategy, software, content and links ourselves and get paid as a percentage of the new revenue the work produces. No growth, no bill.

How big is your share?

We agree it per partnership before kicking off, and it scales with how much needs to be built. It only ever applies to revenue above your existing baseline — never to what you were already earning.

Why not just charge fees?

Hourly billing rewards effort rather than outcomes. A retainer pays out whether or not you grow. By dropping the retainer entirely, our only route to getting paid is making your revenue climb.

How do you attribute the revenue?

We start by agreeing on a baseline from your current numbers and on how new organic revenue is measured — analytics, orders or bookings, depending on the business. Both parties look at the same dashboard.

When does it start paying off?

Search grows slowly, then all at once. Technical work ships within weeks; rankings and revenue usually follow over a few months. Since we finance the ramp-up, the wait costs you nothing.
